Afterglow OS is built upon AOSC OS. Through streamlined features, optimized configurations and curated software selections, Afterglow OS dedicated to providing continuous software and technical updates for various legacy devices. By maintaining a great user experience and performance, it brings old devices back to life and lets users to "rekindle their old love".

::h2{id="features"}
Features
::

### Tailored to the Hardware

Afterglow OS supports devices that are nearly three decades old, such as PCs equipped with 486 processors and Macintosh computers with m68k processors. It also supports relatively newer devices, like Intel Atom netbooks or PowerPC Macs from around 2010. Through configuration tuning and feature tiering, Afterglow OS ensures a smooth and excellent user experience across all types of legacy hardware.

### Continuous Maintenance

The basic requirement for keeping old devices running is continuous and comprehensive software support. Afterglow OS provides continuous feature updates and security patches, allowing you to confidently continue using your legacy devices in various scenarios.

### Looking Toward the Future

We plan to continue maintaining Afterglow OS for years to come and periodically release update packages, allow you to relievedly continue enjoying and playing with the computers that once brought you joy and hold your early memories.
