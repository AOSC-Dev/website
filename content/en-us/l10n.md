---
title: Software Localization
---

As a community primarily composed of native Chinese speakers, we place great importance on the localization (L10n) of software interfaces and user experience. Since the founding of our community many years ago, alongside focusing on localizing our own project interfaces and documentation, we have long been committed to localization work for upstream projects such as GNOME, KDE, CUPS, and Wine, improving the Simplified Chinese user experience across various software.

While advancing this work, we have also taken on the responsibility of editing and maintaining the "Simplified Chinese Free Software Localization Guidelines in Chinese Mainland", a standardization document that guides fellow contributors in carrying out localization work correctly and efficiently. Additionally, since 2020, we have offered localization internship projects through the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences' "Summer of Open Source" program, attracting newcomers from all walks of life to participate in our community's localization efforts.
