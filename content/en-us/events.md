title: Community Events
---

In addition to our daily development activities, our community regularly organizes various of online and offline events to foster a vibrant community atmosphere and share community resources. As an open-source community primarily active on the internet, these events also help strengthen interpersonal relationships and give concrete form to the concepts of community contribution and participation. Our community holds annual summer gatherings called AOSCC every year since 2015, inviting community contributors, members, campus clubs, businesses, and individuals to participate. While promoting our community's work to the outside world, AOSCC provides a platform for hobbists and industry representatives to share technical skills and industry knowledge and trends. Starting in 2024, we have also organized a "Pancake Party" during Chinese New Year, sharing the festive atmosphere with community friends and planning goals for the coming year.

In addition, our community actively organizes "AOSC Campus Tour" events in collaboration with university clubs, and also participates in many exchange events organized by other organizations like communities and clubs, in order to strengthen ties between our community and various local organizations, communities and industry groups, and to make active efforts toward eliminating knowledge barriers and information gaps.

::h2{id="aoscc"}
AOSCC: Annual Community Gathering
::

AOSCC is our community's annual offline gathering, open to all community friends. During the event, we invite community contributors, university club members and industry representatives to share and exchange ideas about their work, and organize fun activities such as prize drawings and codename voting. In recent years, AOSCC has been co-organized by Chinese university clubs, attracting over 100 attendees each year. ::h2{id="campus"}
AOSC Campus Tour
::

The Anthon open source community was born on campus, and supporting the development of campus and grassroots open-source communities and organizations has always been one of our founding aspirations. Today, university clubs remain a key focus of our community's attention and support. Since 2024, we have collaborated with numerous university clubs to organize the "AOSC Campus Tour", inviting community contributors and industry representatives to go into campuses and share community and professional experience, helping more students engage with, understand, and integrate into the open-source community and industry frontiers.

Chinese New Year Pancake Party
::

Starting in 2023, in order to more timely show and communicate our community's work progress and plans, we began organizing online "Pancake Parties" (means "drawing pancakes") during the Chinese New Year holiday, inviting contributors and community friends to discuss achievements, plans and expectations together.
