AOSC OS is a Linux distribution designed and maintained with the goal of being "simple and reliable." Primarily targeting users with some Linux experience, the system is optimized for personal desktop devices, striving to provide an out-of-the-box, simple, and reliable working environment.

::h2{id="features"}
Features
::

### Productive Work

Designed with an out-of-the-box experience as a primary goal, AOSC OS is your capable assistant for daily work. From text work to server administration, from photo editing to 3D modeling, and from classroom note-taking to document translation, our system helps you deploy quickly, work efficiently, and create freely.

### Easy Management

From installation to system updates, and from package management to daily maintenance, AOSC OS has many user-friendly tools built-in to help you eaily manage your system and enjoy using it. For instance, the built-in oma package manager is featured with a concise and intuitive TUI, which makes system management as safe, efficient, and reliable as possible.

### High Compatibility

We believe that operating systems are stages for applications and productivity. Besides officially verified software provided in our repositories, AOSC OS supports cross-distribution ecosystems such as Linglong, Flatpak, Snap and AppImage, broadening the scope of software availability. Furthermore, we provide compatibility with Debian and Ubuntu packages by adding Spiral compatibility tags.

### Available Everywhere

Built upon the highly flexible Linux kernel, AOSC OS supports a wide range of devices across various mainstream processor architectures, including desktops, laptops, single-board computers, servers and etc. Tuned for diverse use cases, AOSC OS helps you unleash the full potential of your hardware.

### Always Here to Help

Our community is renowned for its friendly and responsible support. Respecting users' time and effort is our guiding principle, and the community serves as a strong backbone for information sharing. Across our community chat groups, we are always ready to solve your problems.
